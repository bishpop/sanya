Free 1024×768 XY chain charting and 30-column SLK export.

01 · Shooting Star Studio Showcase

Sculpt spatial chains directly on the 1024×768 stage.

Place orbs with free XY drag, auto-generate geometric Heart, Diamond, Flower, and Butterfly trails, and preview live orb travel.

  1. Free XY Canvas & Geometric Shapes

    Click and drag notes freely across the 1024×768 stage (or snap 8–64px), use Free Spacebar X/Y placement when simultaneous, and stamp 6 new pattern shapes incl. Heart, Diamond, Flower & Butterfly.

  2. Configurable 1–8 MADI Chains & Trail Visuals

    Control 1–8 MADI Start/End chain cadence chart-wide or inside BeatUp conversion, pick Random vs. Continue chain starts, and customize Curved/Dashed/Glow arrow trails.

  3. Lossless 30-Column _star.slk & F1/F2/F3 Views

    Exports all 30 client columns (X1..X30 incl. F/H/A Finish/Hidden/AutoTrigger flags and coordinates), matches shootingstarmusic.slk offsets, and plays original hitsounds.

02 · Architecture & Capabilities

Engineered for speed and format accuracy.

Core capabilities built directly into Shooting Star Studio v0.3.0.0 inside the private Engine Suite.

  1. 01

    1024×768 Canvas & Simultaneous Space XY (v0.3)

    Click and drag notes anywhere across the stage, align Spacebar X/Y independently when simultaneous, and toggle optional 8px–64px grid snaps.

  2. 02

    Configurable 1–8 MADI Chain Engine

    Set custom 1–8 MADI Start/End chain cadences across the entire chart or during BeatUp-to-Shooting-Star conversions, choosing Random or Continue start placement.

  3. 03

    6 Geometric Pattern Shapes & TrailFX

    Stamp clean spatial formations including Heart, Diamond, Flower, and Butterfly directly onto the canvas with customizable Curved, Dashed, or Glow arrow paths.

  4. 04

    Lossless 30-Column _star.slk Roundtrip

    Serializes all 30 SYLK columns (X1 index through X30 radius/mode values) with exact client center-point geometry and F/H/A special flags.

  5. 05

    1:1 Client HUD, Rings & Hitsounds

    Reproduces rotating note rings, cursor interpolation, hit bursts (star_perfect_ef), and original wav feedback inside F1 Gameplay, F2 Editor, or F3 Split View.
