03 · Core Concept

The #1 Rule: Every Note Needs a Start & End!

Why Start and End markers are the foundation of Shooting Star — and what happens without them.

Think of a chain as one connected trail of stars in-game

The cursor flies from star to star. So the game knows WHERE a trail begins and WHERE it disappears again, EVERY group of notes on a lane needs exactly ONE Start marker before it and ONE End marker after it.

✓ RIGHT · Cleanly Closed Chain
1-Str (Start) → #1 [7] → #2 [9] → #3 [4] → 1-End (End)
  • Start (2–4 ticks before Note #1): Gives the player clear sight-reading of where the star flies in from.
  • Notes in between: All L1 notes show a continuous Cyan chain bar along their left edge.
  • End (1–2 ticks after last note): The star trail dissolves cleanly right after the final hit.
  • Tip — Wrap MADI: One click on [Wrap MADI] automatically places Start & End around the current measure!
✕ WRONG · Open / Unwrapped Notes
NO Start! → #1 [7] ! → #2 [9] ! → NO End!
  • Red border in the sequencer: The editor warns you immediately with a red outline & Validator error.
  • Note without Start: In the real game, notes outside a chain DO NOT APPEAR AT ALL!
  • Start without End: The trail stays open forever — later starts on the same lane cause errors.
  • End without Start: A lone End has nothing to close and triggers an Orphan End error.

04 · L1 & L2 System

L1 vs. L2: When to Use Start 1 (1-Str) & Start 2 (2-Str)

The secret to smooth Audition charts: two separate arrow lanes handing off seamlessly.

How L1 and L2 Overlap Cleanly (Time Flows Left → Right)

1-StrNoteNoteNote1-End
L2 (Green)
2-Str / 2-End
2-Str (Overlap OK)NoteNoteNote2-End

When is L1 alone enough?

If there is plenty of breathing room between two phrases (at least 4–6 ticks after 1-End), you can simply start the next phrase on L1 again (1-Str).

When MUST you use L2?

Whenever the next phrase needs to fly onto screen WHILE L1 is still playing or just finishing — start the new phrase with 2-Str on L2 while L1 finishes up to 1-End.

Pro Tip: Alternate L1 ↔ L2

Pro charts alternate between L1 and L2 almost every measure (or every 2 MADIs). That way the player always sees the upcoming star shape in time before the first hit!

05 · Avoid Mistakes

What Happens When L1 & L2 Are Charted Wrong?

The 3 most common lane mistakes — explained clearly with real editor scenarios.

Mistake 1

Double Start on the Same Lane

You place 1-Str on L1 while the previous L1 chain has NOT been closed with 1-End yet.

What happens in-editor & in-game?
  • Validator reports: 'Nested chain start on Track 2'.
  • The active L1 trail cuts off abruptly or notes vanish in-game.
How to fix it:
  • Either close L1 first with 1-End — OR start the new phrase cleanly on L2 (2-Str)!
Mistake 2

End Placed on the Wrong Lane

L1 keeps running across MADI 12–13 while L2 plays briefly — but you accidentally place 1-End instead of 2-End.

What happens in-editor & in-game?
  • L1 gets cut off mid-phrase → all remaining L1 notes glow RED (outside a chain)!
  • Meanwhile L2 stays open forever.
How to fix it:
  • Always match the color: Cyan notes (L1) close ONLY with 1-End, Green notes (L2) ONLY with 2-End!
Mistake 3

Two Arrows on the Exact Same Tick

Both a playable L1 arrow note and a playable L2 arrow note sit on the exact same beat.

What happens in-editor & in-game?
  • Validator warns: 'Two arrow stars on the same tick'.
  • The player would have to hit two directional arrows at once — unfair and miss-prone.
How to fix it:
  • L1 and L2 may overlap through their Start/End markers, but playable arrow notes belong on separate beats!

06 · Spacebar Chains (Hard 6K)

Space Lanes (S1 & S2): Own Start, Own End!

Why Space notes work completely independently from arrow notes — and how to build Space-only sections.

The Golden Space Rule

Spacebar stars belong to their OWN chain (S-Str → Space → S-End).

  • Dedicated Start & End required: Even if L1 is already open with 1-Str, every Space phrase needs its own S-Str before and S-End after!
  • Same beat as arrows allowed: A Space note can sit on the exact same beat as an L1 or L2 arrow note — the player hits Arrow + Spacebar together.
  • Pure Space-Only sections: You can chart sections with NO arrow notes at all (just S-Str → Space notes → S-End). In-game, only golden Space stars appear.
  • S1 & S2 just like L1 & L2: Switch between [S1] (Gold) and [S2] (Orange) in the sequencer toolbar whenever two Space phrases need to overlap.
  • 1-Click editing (v0.6): A single click on S-Str or S-End immediately places or removes the Space marker — whether S1 or S2.
Example A · Arrow + Space

Both hit on the exact same beat — but with their own chains:

1-Str → L1 Note (M8.04) → 1-End
S-Str → Space (M8.04) → S-End

✓ Keep at least 42 px spacing between Arrow & Space on the stage!

✕ Common Space Mistake

Placing a Space note inside an active L1 chain WITHOUT its own S-Str and S-End in the Space columns:

  • Result: The Space note gets a red border ('Space outside Space chain') and WILL NOT appear in-game!
  • Fix: Place S-Str before the first Space note and S-End after the last one (or click [Wrap MADI]).

07 · Special Effects (FX)

Using Finish (F), Hidden (H) & Auto (A) Properly

Add highlights, tension, and variety to your chart — without unexpected side effects.

Column 10 · Key F

Finish Move (Fin)

  • In-game effect: Golden Finish ring with massive bonus points (+1,500 to +4,500 pts) and an intro chime.
  • Piano-Roll drag: Hold left-click and drag down in the Fin column to paint whole Finish streaks (right-drag erases).
  • Watch out: Never place within the first 8 ticks (intro sound gets cut off). Works on BOTH Arrow and Space notes!

Column 11 · Key H

Hidden Star (Hid)

  • In-game effect: The star fades invisible right before the hit and plays a distinct audio cue.
  • Reward & fairness: Awards 10× base points! Any valid mode key counts — and missing it does NOT break your combo!
  • Best practice: Use sparingly on clear musical accents (like drum drops) so players can time it by ear.

Column 12 · Key G

Auto-Trigger (Auto)

  • In-game effect: When the player hits the green Auto-Trigger star, the NEXT starting arrow chain plays automatically!
  • Where to place: Always place it on a note near the end of a phrase, right BEFORE the next arrow chain begins with 1-Str / 2-Str.
  • Watch out: If no new arrow chain starts after it (no 1-Str / 2-Str), Auto will never trigger!
Attachment Rule

Finish (F), Hidden (H), and Auto (A) always attach to an EXISTING note on the same beat — they never spawn ghost arrows!

08 · Stage & Patterns

Building Readable Paths & Using Geometric Patterns

Make your charts not only valid, but genuinely fun and intuitive to read and play.

Good Stage Paths — 5 Practical Rules

  • Steady travel speed (~32 px/tick): Keep distance proportional to beat spacing. Huge cross-screen jumps on fast notes are hard to read!
  • Safe margin zone (X: 60–960, Y: 60–680): Keep stars away from outer edges so rings, arrows, and effects never get clipped.
  • 3 distinct paths in the Stage Viewer: L1 shows in Cyan, L2 in Green, and Space dashed in Gold. You can also drag START and END anchors directly!
  • Fast stage controls: Drag note = move · Mouse Wheel over note = rotate arrow · Right-click note = cycle F/H/A · Box-Drag = multi-select.
  • Avoid stacking: Don't pile notes of the same phrase on top of each other — smooth 2D curves feel way better than stiff straight lines!

09 · Mistake Checklist

Top 6 Charting Mistakes — Symptom, Impact & Quick Fix

Whenever the red or yellow warning bar pops up in the editor, find the instant fix right here.

Symptom / Warning What Happens In-Game? How to Fix It Immediately
Red border on Note / Space Note sits outside a Start..End chain → does not spawn in-game at all! Place the matching Start (1-Str / 2-Str / S-Str) before & End after — or click [Wrap MADI].
Notes in MADI 0 (Tick < 16) Stars spawn during the Ready / Start / 3-2-1 countdown and are barely hittable. Start all playable notes at MADI 2 or MADI 3.
1-tick gap (back-to-back 1/16) Timing windows of two arrows overlap → players get unfair accidental misses. Leave at least 2 ticks (1/8 spacing) between consecutive arrow notes whenever possible.
Arrow & Space stacked together Stars are < 42 px apart → the arrow direction gets hidden underneath. Drag the Space star freely away on the stage (Free Spacebar Position is enabled).
Invalid direction for mode e.g. diagonal arrows (1/3/7/9) in Normal 4K → key doesn't exist in that mode! Select the right mode at the top of the Inspector (Hard 6K for 7/4/1/3/6/9 + Space).
Auto-Trigger (A) doesn't fire Auto only activates on the NEXT starting arrow chain — nothing happens without a new Start. Start the next phrase cleanly with 1-Str or 2-Str after the Auto-Trigger note.

10 · Projects & Shortcuts

.star Project Files, Auto-Save & Keyboard Shortcuts

Work safely like in BeatUp Engine — with embedded audio and identical shortcuts.

.star Project vs. .slk Chart

  • .star Project (Ctrl+Shift+S): Embeds the ENTIRE SONG directly inside the file + chart, BPM, offset & all settings. Opening the .star loads song & chart instantly — even on another PC!
  • .slk Chart (Ctrl+S): Exports the final Audition chart file ready for the game.

Auto-Save & Exit Protection

  • Folder Auto-Save: Configurable under File → Auto-Save (1 / 2 / 5 min, default: 2 min). Automatically saves backups in the same folder as your .ogg or .star file!
  • Exit Confirmation: Closing the app always prompts you cleanly — with one-click Save (.star) or Save (.slk) buttons.

11 · Final Checklist

Pre-Export Checklist — Ready to Play!

Run through these 5 quick checks before sharing your chart or dropping it into the game.

  1. 1

    Validator in the top-right shows GREEN ('Valid')

    Zero red errors! Every L1, L2, and Space note sits cleanly between a matching Start and End.

  2. 2

    Full playthrough in Autoplay (F4)

    Watch the entire song in Autoplay: Are BPM & offset locked in? Do all paths read smoothly? Do Finish, Hidden, and Auto stars fire on beat?

  3. 3

    Playtest it yourself in Gameplay Mode (F1)

    A chart that looks great in Autoplay also needs to feel comfortable on the keyboard — test fast transitions and Arrow + Space combos!

  4. 4

    Save your working project as .star (Ctrl+Shift+S)

    Keeps your audio, chart, and settings safely bundled in a single portable file for future edits.

  5. 5

    Export the final chart as .slk (Ctrl+S)

    Your exported .slk file is 100% Audition-compatible and ready to drop straight into the game!
