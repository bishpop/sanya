Charting Guide · Shooting Star

How to chart Shooting Star.

Shooting Star is a spatial sequence mode where a glowing guide orb travels from star to star across a 1024 × 768 stage. Good charting balances clean rhythm phrasing, readable screen paths, and proper intro/chain pacing.

01 · Workflow

From audio track to playable chart.

Follow these steps in Shooting Star Studio to build a chart quickly without spending hours fixing layouts by hand.

  1. 01

    Set BPM & Beat Offset

    Load your song and detect or enter its BPM (2–360 BPM). Shooting Star uses 16 steps per MADI (4 steps per beat). Check that beats line up cleanly with the waveform—Studio defaults Beat Offset to 0.70 beats, which you can fine-tune by ear with the metronome.

  2. 02

    Pick your target difficulty first

    Select Easy 2K, Normal 4K, or Hard 6K before writing patterns. Each difficulty allows a specific set of arrow directions and decides whether Spacebar stars are allowed.

  3. 03

    Begin at MADI 5 (or MADI 4+)

    Always leave the opening bars empty so the game can display READY → START → 3-2-1 cleanly before the first star arrives.

  4. 04

    Place arrows, Spacebars & shapes

    Place directional stars on the Beat Grid and arrange their stage positions visually. Select ranges of notes and apply built-in geometric patterns (Line, Circle, Star, Wave, Spiral) with Limit Speed turned ON so the orb never teleports wildly.

  5. 05

    Compile chains & test in Gameplay

    Run Compile Chains so every phrase opens with an 8-step lead-in and alternates lanes cleanly. Check that the validator shows 0 Errors, then press F1 with Autoplay (F4) to watch the full flow before saving.

02 · Difficulty Modes

Allowed keys per difficulty.

Each difficulty supports only specific arrow keys. Using a Hard-only diagonal arrow inside Normal 4K causes stars to disappear in-game unless mapped back to 4K.

Mode Overview

ModeAllowed DirectionsSpacebar Stars?
Easy 2KLeft side (4 / Left) & Right side (6 / Right)No (not allowed)
Normal 4KStrictly 4 cardinal directions: Left (4), Up (8), Down (2), Right (6). Avoid 1 / 3 diagonals.No (not allowed)
Hard 6KAll 6 Numpad directions: 7, 4, 1, 3, 6, 9Yes (Spacebar / LCtrl / RCtrl)
Watch out when downgrading Hard to Normal

Down-Left (1) and Down-Right (3) only render in Hard 6K. If you convert a Hard chart to Normal 4K, convert diagonals into standard Up/Down/Left/Right arrows and remove Spacebar stars.

03 · Must-Follow Rules

Rules that keep your chart playable in-game.

Ignoring intro timing or chain spacing is the #1 reason custom Shooting Star charts fail to spawn arrows or break mid-song.

1. Never start before MADI 4–5

The game announces READY, then START (about 2 bars before your phrase opens), then 3 - 2 - 1 right before the guide orb spawns.

If you place your opening star too early (around MADI 0–2), there is not enough room for the intro countdown, and the game can stay stuck on READY without ever spawning stars.

Safe Opening Position

Always start your first playable note at MADI 5 (or at the very earliest MADI 4). That guarantees the 8-step approach lead-in and full countdown play reliably.

2. Phrases (Chains) & Lane Alternation

Playable stars always belong inside a closed phrase with a Start marker and an End marker. Arrow stars and Spacebar stars each use their own separate phrases.

  • Give a 2-beat lead-in: Place Start markers about 8 steps (2 beats) before the phrase's first star, and End markers 1–2 steps after the final star.
  • Alternate Track 1 ↔ Track 2: Because the next phrase begins loading 8 steps before its first note, consecutive phrases must alternate between Lane 1 and Lane 2 so they don't overlap on the same track.
Tip: Let the editor compile chains for you

You can chart freely in Lane 1 and click Compile Chains (or save your chart) so Shooting Star Studio automatically inserts proper 8-step lead-ins and alternates Lane 1 ↔ Lane 2 without gaps or collisions.

04 · Stage & Pacing

Positioning stars, orb speed & Spacebar sync.

How smoothly players can read your chart depends directly on where stars sit on the 1024 × 768 stage and how fast the comet travels between them.

Keep inside the Safe Zone

Although the full stage is 1024 × 768, edges hold the Combo display and score HUD. Keep every star within the outer limit (X 24–992, Y 24–720), and aim for the Comfort Zone (X 120–884, Y 115–615) so approach rings never clip off-screen.

Control Orb Travel Speed

Avoid placing two consecutive fast 1/16 or 1/8 stars on opposite sides of the screen. As a rule of thumb, keep step distance proportional to timing (about 32 px per 1/16 step max). Leave Limit Speed enabled when using shape generators.

Simultaneous Arrow + Space

When an Arrow star and a Spacebar star share the exact same beat, give them the exact same horizontal (X) position and separate them cleanly vertically (at least 64–96 px apart) so neither star covers the other.

05 · Special Stars & Speed Tips

Finish, Hidden, Auto & fast Studio shortcuts.

Use special star types purposefully to highlight choruses and climax beats without blinding the player.

Special Star Types

TypeWhen & How to Use
Finish (F)Highlights climax phrases with golden visuals and bonus points. Attach Finish to Arrow stars (not standalone Spacebars) and give the first Finish note at least half a bar from song start so its intro sound plays cleanly once per Finish span.
Hidden (H)Hides the arrow direction until right before impact. Use sparingly on predictable musical paths so players aren't ambushed mid-stream.
Auto (A)Automatically hits without breaking combo—handy for decorative visual transitions or orb rest beats.

Fast Charting Shortcuts

  • Numpad + Shift: Place a star direction and jump forward by your current Snap step automatically.
  • Mouse Wheel over Stage: Hover any placed star (or the stage) and scroll to rotate its arrow direction instantly.
  • Middle-Click a Star: Jump playback cursor straight to that star's beat.
  • Right-Click a Star: Cycle quickly between Normal, Finish, Hidden, and Auto.
  • Tab Range + Patterns tab: Press Tab at start & end of a phrase, then shape all notes cleanly into a Circle, Wave, Line, or Star with one click.

06 · Final Check

Pre-release Shooting Star checklist.

Run through these six checks in Gameplay Preview (F1 + F4) before sharing your chart.

  • First playable star sits at MADI 4–5 or later so READY → START → 3-2-1 completes cleanly.
  • Validator shows 0 Errors with all arrow and Spacebar phrases properly closed.
  • All stars stay inside the screen comfort zone without clipping HUD counters.
  • Fast sections use natural short distances so the guide orb stays smooth and readable.
  • Any simultaneous Arrow + Space pair shares the same X position with clear vertical spacing.
  • Normal 4K and Easy 2K charts avoid Hard-only diagonals and contain no Spacebar stars.
