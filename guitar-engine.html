01 · Guitar Engine Showcase

Edit timelines on the left. Shred the 3D highway on the right.

Inspect every Strum, No-Strum Tap, Bend sustain, and Special Overdrive phrase live with a frame-accurate AutoPlay bot.

  1. Dual-Pane Split View (F3) & 192-Tick Grid

    Pairs an interactive 2D timeline editor (Lanes 1–4 + Special Overdrive, waveform, drag-resize Long Note tails, Q quantize, M lane mirror) directly beside the real 3D highway.

  2. 3D Perspective Highway & FlameOut Gauge

    Renders the authentic perspective formula (Y = 265 + 439 · s(u)) to receptor line Y=704 with SPARK combo HUD, Strum (Shift), Bend (Space → SUCCESS x2), and ≥500 FlameOut trigger.

  3. v300 CSV Roundtrip & Dual-Track AutoPlay (F2)

    Reads/exports all 13 Guitar v300 CSV note types with auto 3 - fileLane mirroring, streams Backing + Guitar OGG stems simultaneously, and mutes the guitar stem cleanly on misses.

02 · Architecture & Capabilities

Engineered for speed and format accuracy.

Core capabilities built directly into Guitar Engine v0.1.0.0 inside the private Engine Suite.

  1. 01

    Authentic 3D Highway & Original Assets

    Implements the exact perspective highway projection to the Y = 704 receptor bar, complete with original SPARK combo readouts, FlameOut bursts, and miss SFX.

  2. 02

    Dual-Track Backing + Guitar Audio

    Streams Backing.ogg and Guitar.ogg tracks in lockstep via miniaudio; missing notes automatically silences the isolated Guitar stem until you recover the combo.

  3. 03

    Complete Audition v300 CSV Compatibility

    Loads and exports official Guitar v300 CSV charts across all 13 note types with automatic 3 - fileLane layout mirroring.

  4. 04

    Precision AutoPlay Bot (F2)

    Toggle the glowing AUTO pill anytime to watch the bot perform Strum holds, Spacebar Bends (SUCCESS x2), and FlameOut bursts frame-accurately.

  5. 05

    Rich 2D Timeline Editor Tools

    Quantize selected notes (Q), flip/mirror lanes (M), scrub time/measure readouts, adjust speed from 25% to 150%, and toggle views instantaneously with F3.
