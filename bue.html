Chart, play and verify BeatUp charts.

01 · Chart editor

Edit against the running game.

Editor and game share one time base, so a note you place is judged exactly as it will be played.

  1. Waveform and lane grid

    A waveform strip next to the eight columns 7 · 4 · 1 · 9 · 6 · 3 · Space · Finish, with snap grid, beat and MADI markers.

  2. Transport and readout

    Play, pause and seek. BPM, beat, MADI, combo and maximum score update while you work.

  3. Live game view

    The chart runs in the real game view with judgments, score and receptors. Split View keeps editor and game side by side.

02 · Play and patch

Play any chart. Keep your library tidy.

The same engine that you edit with is the one you play on.

  1. Play your chart

    Run the charts in your charts folder straight from the main menu, with inline Delete .bu confirmation and F5 Refresh.

  2. Judgment, combo and score

    Perfect timing is configurable; Great, Cool, Bad and Miss follow refined rules. Chance modes such as Rapid, Vanishing and Wave change how notes arrive.

  3. Receptors and Challenger Mode

    Receptor bar and colours are themeable. The optional Challenger Mode makes runs stricter.

  4. Patcher song library

    Browse songs with rankings, check BPM, length and level, then press Play. The Patcher validates required content and applies each song's timing offset.

03 · Auditor Mode Telemetry 2.0

Check a chart. Show what changed.

Auditor Mode makes chart revisions traceable. It opens in its own native refresh-rate window and detects the BPM of a .slk from the music and the chart.

  1. Inspect

    Open a .slk or .bu. The Auditor lists duplicate arrows, arrow and Space in one row, Finish problems, empty charts, invalid BPM and long gaps. Click an entry to jump to that row.

  2. Correct and compare

    Fix the chart in the normal editor. New notes get a green frame, removed notes a red ghost. The compare view shows original and revision side by side.

  3. Document

    Every change is written to a .csv log next to the chart. In an audit project the original, the revision and the log travel together in one .bu.

04 · Features

What is in the box.

Timing, gameplay, editing, Direct3D 11 Flip Model rendering, the chart assistant, project files, patching and the Auditor in one program.

  1. 01

    Direct3D 11 & 1000Hz Input

    Native Direct3D 11 Flip Model rendering removes modern RTX frame caps while 1000Hz+ polling lowers input-to-draw latency.

  2. 02

    Timing and judgment

    Gameplay graphics and hit detection use one time base, with configurable Perfect timing and refined Great, Cool, Bad and Miss behaviour.

  3. 03

    Chart editor

    Receptor Bar, Split View, vertical or horizontal canvas, waveform, beat and MADI navigation, selection, copy/paste replacement and undo/redo.

  4. 04

    Project files

    .bu projects keep audio, BPM, offset, notes, speed and the saved playback position together for a complete editing session.

  5. 05

    Theme editor

    Change the core interface colours, waveform, minimap and Receptor Bar with a live preview and persistent theme.ini themes.

  6. 06

    Chance and Challenger

    Rapid, Vanishing, Wave, Inverted, Speed Up and Halfway Chance modes, with the optional Challenger Mode for stricter runs.

  7. 07

    Chart assistant

    Detects BPM, beats and transients and proposes an editable chart draft that follows the song, with Spaces on the MADI and sections by intensity.

  8. 08

    Engine diagnostics

    An optional overlay for frame times, audio stalls, input delay, renderer and memory, with an exportable report for hardware diagnostics.

  9. 09

    Auditor Mode & Diff

    Open a .slk or .bu, inspect anomalies, compare original vs. revision side by side, and export semicolon-separated CSV telemetry.

  10. 10

    Engine Launcher Sync

    Shares .bue_session charter identity, profile pill, differential encrypted .enc updates, and inline .bu file controls.
