01 · AuPiano Engine Showcase

Eight piano lanes, hold notes, and instant pattern generation.

Chart live while listening, drag out clean holds, or rebalance tricky chords with anti-jack Random 4K and Random 8K algorithms.

  1. 4K & 8K Key Layouts & Live Record (R)

    Supports full 8-key A S D F · J K L ; (Ö on DE keyboards) plus 4K Inner (D F J K), Left (A S D F), or Split (S D K L), with cursor placement or live Record (R) during playback.

  2. Random 4K/8K & Pattern Generator

    Ctrl+4 / Ctrl+8 remap charts safely with anti-jack protection and hold note preservation; Pattern Generator creates custom practice streams across measure ranges and densities.

  3. Byte-for-Byte X1..X7 .slk & .sanmeta Export

    Preserves exact game SYLK headers while writing s section markers every N measures, f finale sections, and .sanmeta metadata sidecars for BPM/offset persistence.

02 · Architecture & Capabilities

Engineered for speed and format accuracy.

Core capabilities built directly into AuPiano Engine v1.0.1 inside the private Engine Suite.

  1. 01

    CleanUI & 1000 FPS Engine Parity

    Built on C++17, SDL2, Dear ImGui, and miniaudio with BeatUp Engine theming (BeatUp Night, Catppuccin Mocha/Latte, Tokyo Night, Cyberpunk Neon) and configurable FPS up to 1000 FPS.

  2. 02

    Multiple 4K Subsets & Full 8K Mode

    Chart full 8-key piano arrangements (A S D F · J K L ;) or inspect 4K reductions via Inner (D F J K), Left (A S D F), or Split (S D K L) modes.

  3. 03

    Smart Random 4K / 8K & Pattern Generator

    Automatically re-maps charts without impossible repeat jacks while keeping hand alternation natural and holds intact.

  4. 04

    Step Entry, Drag-Holds & Live Recording

    Place taps on 1/4, 1/8, or 1/16 grids using keyboard shortcuts (A..; or 1–8), drag holds down with mouse or keyboard, or record live on the fly.

  5. 05

    Lossless .slk + .sanmeta & ArrowVortex Import

    Imports StepMania dance-double charts cleanly and exports byte-pure Audition Piano .slk files paired with lightweight .sanmeta timing descriptors.
