Charting Guide · Piano Mode

How to chart Piano Mode.

Piano Mode maps melodies, chords, and sustains cleanly across 4-Key or 8-Key home-row lanes (A S D F · J K L ;) with structured sections ending in a Finale phrase.

01 · Keyboard & Lanes

Pitch-ordered lanes from bass to treble.

Map left-hand bass or rhythm accompaniment onto A S D F and right-hand melodies onto J K L ; so players naturally read high and low notes across the keyboard.

8-Key vs. 4-Key Layout

  • 8-Key Mode: Full 8 fingers on home row (A S D F left hand, J K L ; right hand—on German layouts the 8th lane corresponds to Ö).
  • 4-Key Modes: Great for easier difficulties using Inner (D F J K), Left (A S D F), or Split (S D K L).

Step-by-Step or Live Record

You can place notes accurately step by step on 1/4, 1/8, or 1/16 grids, or switch on Live Record (R) with the metronome (M) to play parts live along with the song, then refine hold tails and timing afterward.

02 · Charting Flow

Taps, Holds, Chords & Finale Sections.

Smooth Piano charting treats the keyboard like an instrument—avoid awkward single-finger jacks and honor held notes.

  1. 01

    Drag crisp Hold notes

    Hold keys for sustained vocal syllables, piano chords, or synth pads (minimum 2+ grid steps). Always leave at least one empty grid step after a hold releases before placing another note in that exact same lane.

  2. 02

    Separate Left-Hand Harmony & Right-Hand Melody

    Even during busy chords, avoid stacking two-hand 6+ key mashes. Clean 2-note or 3-note chords (for example 1 bass hold in A/S while J/K/L plays the melody) feel much more musical than wall-of-notes spam.

  3. 03

    Use Anti-Jack Finger Switching

    When a fast vocal or piano note repeats on the same pitch, alternate gently between adjacent fingers (such as J → K → J) or use SanPiano's Random 4K/8K anti-jack balancing.

  4. 04

    Frame Sections & Finale Cleanly

    Piano songs progress in regular sections (commonly every 8 measures) leading into a closing Finale section for the outro climax. Check your Section intervals before exporting.

03 · Final Check

Pre-test Piano checklist.

Run a quick playtest across both 4K and 8K before sharing your chart.

  • BPM and Measure 0 offset match the first downbeat on the metronome.
  • Hold notes do not collide with new notes on the same lane before releasing.
  • Both hands carry a balanced workload across verses and choruses.
  • Repeating notes avoid tiring rapid single-finger jacks.
  • Sections advance evenly and end with a dedicated Finale phrase.
  • 4K charts stick cleanly to four lanes without stray notes outside the active subset.
