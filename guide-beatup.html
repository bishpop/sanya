Charting Guide · BeatUp

How to chart BeatUp.

BeatUp combines converging left-and-right note lanes with Spacebar accents and Finish Moves. Great BeatUp charts match the song's rhythm clearly while keeping both hands moving comfortably across 7 · 4 · 1 and 9 · 6 · 3.

01 · Lane System

Eight columns, one center receptor.

In BeatUp, notes fly inward from the left and right edges toward the central hit zone. Left-side arrows correspond to 7 · 4 · 1, right-side arrows to 9 · 6 · 3, and Space/Finish sit in their own dedicated lanes.

Left & Right Playable Lanes

SideLanesMusical Role
Left Side7 (Top) · 4 (Mid) · 1 (Bottom)Left-hand melodies, bass hits, descending or ascending runs
Right Side9 (Top) · 6 (Mid) · 3 (Bottom)Right-hand counter-melodies, alternating crossovers, echoes
Space LaneSpaceStrong snare claps, vocal punches, or measure accents
Finish LaneFinishPhrase endings, drops, and chorus transitions

4-Key vs. 6-Key Approach

6-Key BeatUp lets you assign exact lanes (7 · 4 · 1 · 9 · 6 · 3) for deliberate stairs, crossovers, and visual symmetry.

4-Key / Random styles focus purely on timing, Spacebar bursts, and balanced left/right groups—ideal for easier charts or when using tools like SanConverter to distribute finger-friendly patterns automatically.

Readability First

Use Top (7 / 9) for higher pitches, Mid (4 / 6) for steady groove notes, and Bottom (1 / 3) for heavier low beats so players instinctively feel the melody visually.

02 · Timing Preparation

Lock BPM and Offset before placing a single note.

No pattern feels good if the audio drifts. Calibrate your timing map first using BeatUp Engine's waveform view and metronome.

  1. 01

    Verify true BPM

    Use Beat Detection or tap along to double-check that the song isn't charted at half-time or double-time. Steady grid alignment across both the intro and the final chorus proves your BPM is exact.

  2. 02

    Dial in Song Offset

    Line up Measure 0 (or the very first strong downbeat) directly with the kick peak on the waveform. Always confirm with BeatUp Engine's Play / Live Split View and assist ticks.

  3. 03

    Use appropriate Snap values

    Chart normal grooves on 1/4 and 1/8 snaps, reserve 1/16 for fast runs and fills, and only switch to 1/12 or 1/24 when the song actually swings in triplets.

  4. 04

    Leave a clean intro

    Give the player at least 1–2 empty measures at the very start before notes arrive so they have time to orient their hands when the track begins.

03 · Patterns & Flow

Build patterns that feel natural to play.

The difference between an exhausting chart and a memorable BeatUp chart comes down to hand alternation and pattern consistency.

Cap Repeated Jacks

Pressing the exact same lane (for example 4 → 4 → 4 → 4) quickly strains fingers and breaks flow. Keep intentional jacks to at most 2–3 hits unless the song has an unmistakable staccato synth hit.

Stairs & Rolls

Stepping cleanly through 7 → 4 → 1 or alternating 7 → 9 → 4 → 6 → 1 → 3 feels rhythmic and rewarding. Keep repeating musical motifs shaped consistently across choruses.

Double Notes Wisely

Simultaneous Left + Right chords (such as 4 + 6 or 7 + 9) emphasize heavy drops cleanly. Avoid placing 3 or more arrows on the exact same row—clean BeatUp charts keep rows playable at 1–2 arrows.

04 · Spacebar & Finish

Never mix Arrow + Space on the same row.

Spacebar and Finish Moves are the signature accents of BeatUp—when paced cleanly around arrows instead of stacked on top of them.

Spacebar Rules

  • Give Space its own beat: Never place an arrow note and a Spacebar on the exact same row unless intentionally testing special edge behavior; normal charts give Space its own crisp hit on the snare or off-beat.
  • Create rhythmic bursts: Alternating Arrow → Space → Arrow → Space builds classic BeatUp groove during rap verses or percussive breakdowns.

Finish Moves & Density Pacing

  • Mark musical resolutions: Place Finish Moves at the end of 4-bar or 8-bar phrases rather than spamming them every measure.
  • Watch empty gaps: Avoid leaving awkward 15–20 second breaks without cues; if a song has a quiet bridge, simplify to light 1/4 beat taps or gentle left/right pulses.

05 · Quality Review

Audit & playtest before publishing.

Use BeatUp Engine's Auditor Mode and Live Play to catch layout errors automatically before you finish.

  • BPM and offset stay locked in sync from the first beat all the way to the outro.
  • No row contains 3+ arrows stacked on the exact same beat.
  • Arrow notes and Spacebars never share the same row unintentionally.
  • Jacks stay controlled (1–2 repeats max on fast tempos) and stairs match the melody's direction.
  • Finish Moves cleanly punctuate major phrases and chorus drops.
  • Full playthrough completed without Auditor errors or awkward long dead zones.
