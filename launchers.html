02 · Distribution Policy

Why Access Is Restricted.

Clear separation between public community software and hardware-locked Engine Suite builds.

Strictly Closed HWID & Manifest Delivery

All 8 suite modules (including BeatUp Engine v1.3+ builds) are delivered solely as encrypted .enc archives through the Engine Launcher once an account and PC Hardware ID are approved. This guarantees matching formats across tools, clean dependency trees, and curated telemetry during private testing.

No standalone ZIPs, mirrors, or public binaries are provided for restricted modules.
