02 · Distribution Policy

100% Private Software Distribution.

Every binary in the Sanya catalog is privately maintained. Documentation and mode charting guides remain open for study.

Strictly Closed HWID & Encrypted Delivery

All suite builds are delivered solely as encrypted .enc archives through the Engine Launcher once an account and PC Hardware ID are approved. No standalone ZIPs, installers, mirrors, or public binaries are provided for any project.
