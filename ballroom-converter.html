01 · Ballroom Converter Workflow Showcase

Pick a song and style. Output authentic choreography.

Audition Ballroom charts organize animation move pools rather than raw timing ticks — SanBallroom enforces every game constraint automatically.

  1. SanSuite Hub & 7 Move Pools

    Detects length, title, and artist via audio metadata tags (.ogg/.mp3/.wav/.flac) and maps ascending move progressions strictly within each style's 043/model/ cap.

  2. 6-Step Level Climbing & Classic Line Math

    Writes b l r level-0 opening rows, builds 6-step blocks climbing levels 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 (each step lasting 2 measures), and enforces Classic vs Couple Hello line counts.

  3. Spreadsheet Inspector & Music Row Export

    Inspect SLK cells side by side like Excel / Pochita, reposition Hello markers in place, customize moves, and copy tab-separated rows straight into ballroommusic.slk.

02 · Architecture & Capabilities

Engineered for speed and format accuracy.

Core capabilities built directly into Ballroom Converter v1.1.1 inside the private Engine Suite.

  1. 01

    Exact Audition SYLK & CP949 Headers

    Preserves the byte-level Korean CP949 SYLK header rows and column layout so generated <name>_s.slk files load cleanly in the Audition client without encoding corruption.

  2. 02

    Bounded 7-Style Move Pools

    Automatically enforces the exact upper animation index inside 043/model/<folder>/ for Cha Cha Cha (37), Jive (30), Paso Doble (22), Rumba (32), Salsa 1 (49), Salsa 2 (45), and Samba (30).

  3. 03

    Classic vs. Couple Hello Parity (v1.1)

    Implements the exact v1.1 discovery ensuring every Hello marker consumes one line fewer in Classic mode compared to Couple mode, eliminating desync.

  4. 04

    Side-by-Side Spreadsheet Inspector

    Inspect compiled SYLK rows alongside Pochita/Excel structures, reposition Hello blocks inline, and override individual male/female (.rgm) dance moves.

  5. 05

    Automatic Track Metadata & Songlist Helper

    Reads BPM, track length, title, and artist from Vorbis/ID3 tags (falling back to Artist - Title filenames) and formats tab-separated lines for ballroommusic.slk.
