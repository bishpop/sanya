01

1:1 Timing Engine

Graphics and hit logic share one time base. Judgment follows the original — no note snapping, every window reachable.

02

Authentic Layout

Numpad 9/6/3, 7/4/1, Space and Finish Move controls with Mirror and AutoPlay support.

03

Theme Editor

Six base colors style the whole app, with a live preview and your own themes saved to theme.ini.

04

Chart Editor

1/4, 1/8 and 1/16 snapping, a resizable split view, waveform and beat-by-beat navigation.

05

.bu Project Files

Save audio, BPM, position and notes and reopen the whole editing session later.

06

Patcher Chances

Rapid, Vanishing, Wave, Inverted, Speed Up and Halfway — plus Challenger Mode, Enter Lock and Instant Play.

Patch Notes · v0.6.1

  • Reworked hit judgment — no note snapping, Great reliably hittable.
  • Theme Editor with live preview and saved themes.
  • Resizable split view that zooms the game to fit.
  • New onboarding, animated main menu and categorised Options.
  • Enter Lock, Instant Play and a Shadows setting.
  • New player with hover time preview; arrow keys jump by beat.
  • .bu projects resume the music at the saved position.
