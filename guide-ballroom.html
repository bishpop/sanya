Charting Guide · Ballroom

How to chart Ballroom Mode.

Instead of placing individual arrow ticks, Ballroom charting pairs a song's BPM, offset, and intro length with an ordered choreography sequence—opening with ready poses, cycling smoothly through Level 6–9 dance steps, and finishing with a Finale pose.

01 · How a Ballroom Chart Works

Every step spans two measures of music.

Getting Ballroom pacing right starts with counting measures accurately from your song's BPM and downbeat offset.

  1. 01

    Calibrate BPM & Beat Offset first

    Because Ballroom steps trigger strictly in 2-measure blocks, even a slight BPM drift shifts the partner poses off-beat later in the song. Measure the true BPM across the chorus before generating moves.

  2. 02

    Set Intro Measures (4–17 bars)

    Count how many empty measures the track needs before the main groove begins. Standard songs use between 4 and 17 intro measures so the dancers enter cleanly on the first main verse.

  3. 03

    Open with Basic & Ready Poses

    Every sequence begins with the three opening poses (Basic Pose → Left Ready → Right Ready) before entering the main Level 6–9 choreography rounds.

  4. 04

    Progress in 6-Step Blocks (Lv 6 → 7 → 8 → 9)

    Steps advance in clean blocks of six—climbing from Level 6 through Level 9 and cycling back—and conclude with a single Finale move timed so it completes right before the song ends.

02 · The 7 Dance Styles

Pick the choreography pool that fits the song's feel.

Each dance style brings its own animation pool size and Finale flourish. Match the style to the rhythm and tempo of your song.

03 · Final Check

Pre-test Ballroom checklist.

Confirm these details before testing your chart in-game.

  • BPM and song offset stay dead-on across the entire track length.
  • Intro measure count lands your first Level 6 move cleanly on Beat 1 of the verse.
  • Step count leaves enough room at the end of the track for the Finale move without cutting short.
  • Chosen dance style matches the tempo and energy of the audio.
